## 2. Core Governance Principles

1. **Workspace-Wide Authority:** Standards documented here govern all projects in `02_Projects/` and reusable content in `03_Knowledge_Base/`.
2. **Lean & Traceable:** Avoid bureaucracy for its own sake. Rules exist to preserve traceability, consistency, and readability for both human architects and AI systems.
3. **No Overwriting Without Justification:** Never silently overwrite or retire valid knowledge. Follow the procedures defined in [change-management.md](change-management.md).
