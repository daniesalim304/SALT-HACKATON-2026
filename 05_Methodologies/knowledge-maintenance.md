# Knowledge Maintenance Methodology

Hygiene and maintenance guidelines to keep the Second Brain coherent, scalable, and free of dead links or outdated information.

---

## 1. Maintenance Checklist
* **Link Integrity:** Verify relative markdown links between projects, templates, and methodologies.
* **Orphan Detection:** Ensure any file placed in the workspace is linked from at least one parent README or index file.
* **Knowledge Extraction:** Periodically scan completed projects in `02_Projects/` to identify reusable patterns suitable for distillation into `03_Knowledge_Base/`.
* **Inbox Zero:** Ensure raw materials dropped into `01_Inbox/` are classified and moved to their designated project or reference home.
